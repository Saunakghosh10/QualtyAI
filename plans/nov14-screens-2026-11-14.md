# Nov 14 meeting: screens plan

Source: Vinay H call, 2026-11-14. Store and database rules are done (commit d1e5cee, branch `feat/nov14-dashboards`).
Skills used: stageshow (compulsory UI), frontend-design, make-interfaces-feel-better, copywriting, tdd; review with code-review + web-design-guidelines.

## Who sees what

| Person | Lands on | Sees prices as |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor | `/` customer landing, service store | customer price (company price + 30%) |
| Customer | `/`, their orders, `/rooms` | customer price, own budget |
| Inspection company | `/dashboard` (never the customer booking hero) | own price, customer budget − 30% |
| Qualty admin | `/dashboard` with all companies + payments | both, plus price override |
| Company not signed up | `/companies` landing + sign-up | — |

## Slices (each: failing test first where there is logic, then build, then commit)

0. **Store: one price per side.** Add per-side fields (`customerPriceUSD` on bids, rounds and listings, `companyBudgetUSD` on orders) and helpers
   `listingCustomerPrice`, `bidCustomerPrice`, `roundCustomerPrice`, `orderCustomerPrice`, `orderCompanyBudget`.
   Remote fills the side the reader is allowed to see; local computes both. Database: customers stop reading
   `service_listings` directly (owners and admins only); everyone else uses `browse_listings`.
1. **Routing by role.** `useViewer()` → visitor / customer / company / admin. New pages `/dashboard`, `/companies`, `/rooms`.
   Navbar links per role. Company sign-up leaves the homepage for `/companies`.
2. **Company dashboard** (`/dashboard`): company header (name, KYC state, accreditations), then the console:
   enquiries with the reduced budget, service listings (location, price, availability, "Customers pay $X"),
   standard certificate services (ECTN, CoC, Customs Import Control at one rate across India), accreditations
   (NABL, NABCB and others), team. Payments tab shows paid / waiting only; no invoice amounts.
3. **Admin** (same page, admin role): all companies, raise invoice + record payment, set the customer price
   (never below the company's, only before payment).
4. **Customer prices on screen**: service store, booking modal, bids panel in the room.
5. **Chat window** (`/rooms`): WhatsApp-style two panes. Left: groups with the room title (group name · order no.).
   Right: messages under day headers (Today, Yesterday, Tue, 10 Nov 2026), rename group inline.
   Homepage keeps a small "Your chatrooms" entry that opens `/rooms` in a new window.
6. **`/companies` landing**: what Qualty gives inspection companies, one stageshow still of the dashboard
   receiving an enquiry, sign-up.
7. **E2E**: update dollar assertions, add role routing and rename-room flows.
